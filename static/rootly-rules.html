Rootly QC Rules

What an incident is judged on. The scorer, the list and the CSV all read this one config.

Checks

Unchecking a rule removes it from scoring and from the overall grade everywhere — it does not hide past results.

Update cadence (IR5)

Hours without any activity — the incident channel's last message, else the record's last update — before the check fails.

Status freshness (IR6)

Hours since the incident last moved lifecycle stage (approximated from its stage timestamps) before it counts as stuck.

Excluded statuses

One status per line, on top of resolved / closed / cancelled — e.g. in_triage to keep triage noise ungraded. Excluded incidents are not fetched, scored or counted.
