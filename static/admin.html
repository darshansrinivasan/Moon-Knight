Ticket statuses

Which Pylon statuses count as work the team is graded on. An excluded status is out of scope everywhere at once — it is not scored, not counted in the calendar, the day list, the leaderboard or the Slack report, and not billed. It is still fetched and still stored, so excluding a status is reversible and re-including one brings its history back.

The list is built from statuses actually present in fetched tickets, so a new Pylon status appears here on its own once the first ticket carrying it is fetched. Nothing needs a deploy.
